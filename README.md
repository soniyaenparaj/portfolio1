# Freelance Portfolio Samples

**A fictional, self-directed sample portfolio.** Northstar Home is an invented homewares business; all people, orders, prices, campaign details, and business context are fictional. These projects demonstrate sample capabilities and are not client work or real campaign results.

## Case study

**Brief:** Give a fictional homewares studio a consistent seasonal launch across sales reporting, social media, a presentation, and a basic storefront.

**Approach:** Set one visual system (forest green, warm cream, clay, and sage), use one clear campaign idea (“Make room for calm”), then adapt the message to each format. The workbook includes source rows, a cleanup log, and a summary. The presentation explains the audience assumption, message, channels, and useful launch metrics without claiming results.

**Scope:** Four self-directed examples created to demonstrate data organization, visual design, presentation structure, and responsive front-end basics. No real market research or client results are represented.

## Projects

1. **Excel sales analysis** — `01_Sales_Analysis.xlsx` includes a summary, raw source rows, cleaning notes, and a cleaned dataset. Open in Microsoft Excel, LibreOffice Calc, or upload to Google Sheets.
2. **Social media design set** — `02_Social_Designs/` contains three editable 1080 × 1080 SVG campaign designs. Preview the SVG files in a browser; edit with Canva, Figma, or Inkscape.
3. **Campaign presentation** — `03_Campaign_Presentation.pptx` is a six-slide launch concept. Open in PowerPoint, LibreOffice Impress, or Google Slides.
4. **Responsive small-business website** — `04_Website/index.html` and `styles.css`. Open `index.html` in a browser; edit the files in any text editor.

## Before sharing with a client

- Replace this sample identity with your name and contact method.
- Keep the fictional/sample-work labels visible wherever the work is shown.
- Be ready to explain what you made, the fictional brief, and what you would change for a real client.
- Do not present any sample as paid client work or claim the illustrative campaign produced results.
